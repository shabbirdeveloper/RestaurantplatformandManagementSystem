Live platform review, accessed 2026-08-04
- Main public platform: https://www.evotegb.com/
  Public navigation and services include membership, leadership, opportunities, Roundu statistics, public verification, member login and election access.
- Membership overview: https://www.evotegb.com/membership
  The live page describes online application, committee review, approval with a unique membership ID, and portal access. It also lists digital identity, card/certificate, CV and cover-letter tools, opportunities, community and volunteering benefits.
- Election portal: https://www.evotegb.com/election-portal
  The live process describes a six-digit email OTP with a five-minute expiry, member identity confirmation, candidate/profile/manifesto review, one complete ballot submission and a unique receipt code. The portal also exposes election overview and results routes; voting was not yet open at review time.
